What is D invites?
D invites is a platform that lets you create beautiful event invitation pages in minutes. You can share a single link, track RSVPs in real time, and manage your guest list – all from your dashboard.
What is the AI feature and how does it work?
Our AI feature lets you describe your event in plain English – like "My sister Amara is marrying Chidi at Grand Palm Gardens, Lekki on 21st November 2026, noon" – and the AI automatically fills the event title, date, venue, and even suggests a story. It's free to use and saves you time.
Is the AI feature free to use?
Yes. The AI form filler is completely free for all users – no limits, no hidden charges. You can use it as many times as you need.
How do I create an event with AI?
Simply sign up (it's free), go to your dashboard, and click "New event". Then type a short description of your event in the AI box – the AI will fill the title, date, venue, and story for you. Review, tweak if needed, and publish.
Is D invites really free?
Yes, the Free plan gives you one active event with up to 50 RSVPs – completely free, no credit card required. If you need more events or higher limits, you can upgrade to Pro or Premium from your billing page.
How do guests RSVP?
Guests simply open your event link, enter their name and phone number (optional), choose whether they're attending, and optionally leave a message. Their response appears instantly on your dashboard under "Guests & RSVPs".
Do my guests need an account to RSVP?
No. Guests never create an account or log in. They open your link, respond, and that's it – the fastest possible path from receiving an invite to confirming their spot.
Can I customize the look of my event page?
Absolutely. You can upload a cover image, choose from several theme styles (more themes available on Pro and Premium plans), and write your own story. The page is designed to look beautiful on any device.
Can I edit my event after publishing it?
Yes. Open My Events, click the pencil icon on any event, update the details, and save. Changes appear on your live page immediately – no republishing step.
Can I delete an event?
Yes. Click the trash icon on any event card in My Events and confirm. This permanently removes the event and all of its RSVPs, so make sure you've exported your guest list first if you'll need it later.
What image formats can I use for my cover photo?
PNG, JPEG, or WEBP, up to 4MB. Images are hosted separately from your account data, so uploading a cover photo never counts against your plan's event or RSVP limits.
Does D invites work well when shared on WhatsApp?
Yes, it's built for it. When you paste your event link into WhatsApp, it shows a proper preview with your event's real title, photo, and details – not a generic placeholder.
What happens if I exceed the free plan limits?
You'll see a notice in your dashboard when you're near the limit. You can either delete an old event to free up space, or upgrade to Pro or Premium to unlock more events and RSVPs.
How do the referral points work?
You get a unique referral code in your dashboard. When a friend signs up using your code and creates their first event, you earn 50 points and your friend gets 20 points. Points help you level up and unlock perks.
Is my guest data secure?
Yes. Guest names, phone numbers, and RSVP responses are only visible to you – the event host. We use Supabase's built‑in Row Level Security to keep your data private.
Can I export my guest list?
Yes, if you're on a Pro or Premium plan. You'll see an "Export CSV" button on the RSVP page of your dashboard. This downloads a spreadsheet with all guest details.
How do I cancel my subscription?
You can cancel anytime from your billing page. Your plan will stay active until the end of the current billing period, then downgrade to Free. No hidden fees.
What makes D invites different from other invitation sites?
Speed, simplicity, and AI. D invites is built minimal on purpose – no heavy page builder, no bloated templates, no unnecessary steps. Plus, our free AI form filler turns your description into a complete event page in seconds.

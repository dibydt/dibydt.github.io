Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 2026

D invites uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience, analyze usage, and improve our service. This policy explains how we use cookies and your choices.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help us remember your preferences and understand how you interact with our platform.

Types of cookies we use

  • Essential cookies: Necessary for the platform to function (e.g., authentication, session management).
  • Preference cookies: Remember your settings, such as language and theme.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how you use the site (e.g., page views, clicks). We use this data to improve our service.
  • Marketing cookies: Used to deliver relevant advertisements (we currently do not use third‑party marketing cookies, but may in the future).

Your choices

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect functionality.

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes.

Contact

If you have any questions about our cookie usage, please contact us at support.d-invite@devtem.org.

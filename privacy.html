Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

At D invites (“we”, “us”, “our”), we take your privacy seriously. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our AI-powered event invitation platform.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as when you create an account, create an event using AI assistance, or RSVP to an event. This may include:

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • Event details (title, date, venue, guest list)
  • AI-generated event descriptions and story suggestions
  • RSVP responses and messages

2. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our AI-powered services
  • Send you updates about your events and account
  • Help you connect with guests and manage RSVPs
  • Improve our AI form filler and other features
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the platform (e.g., hosting, email delivery, AI processing)
  • Other users only as necessary for event coordination (e.g., guest names and RSVPs are visible to the event host)
  • Law enforcement if required by law

4. AI and your data

When you use our AI form filler, your event description is processed to generate event details. This data is not stored permanently and is not used to train AI models without your explicit consent.

5. Data security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Your rights

You may access, update, or delete your account and personal data at any time through your dashboard. You may also contact us for assistance.

7. Cookies

We use cookies to enhance your experience. For more details, please see our Cookie Policy.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or through the platform.

9. Contact us

If you have any questions, please contact us at support.d-invite@devtem.org.
